## Naming convention
- lowercase only
- words separated by hyphens
- no spaces
- no `(1)`, `final`, `new`, `latest`, or random camera filenames
- keep the exact filenames already provided

Example:
`rajashree-ramamoorthy.png` ✅
`Rajashree Final New.png` ❌

## Important
You do **not** need to edit `App.jsx` when replacing an image. Simply overwrite the corresponding placeholder file with your final image using the exact same filename and path.
